Work and focus

Soheil Mozaffari is a software engineer and systems architect focused on the structural problems that emerge when human teams and AI coding agents work in parallel on shared systems. The Interface-First Execution Methodology was developed to address high-parallelism failure modes: undocumented assumptions, accidental APIs, incompatible schemas, and integration events that become the primary coordination mechanism instead of a final verification step.

The methodology draws on practical work in distributed systems design, contract-driven development, and governance of AI-assisted engineering workflows. It is documented in publications archived on Zenodo with persistent DOIs and is available for reading, citation, and adaptation under open-access terms.
