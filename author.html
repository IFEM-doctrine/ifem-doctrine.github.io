Work and focus

Soheil Mozaffari is a software engineer and systems architect whose work centres on the structural problems that emerge when engineering teams and AI coding agents work in parallel on shared systems. The Interface-First Execution Methodology was developed to address the coordination failure modes that appear in high-parallelism environments: undocumented assumptions, accidental APIs, incompatible schemas, and integration events that function as the primary coordination mechanism rather than as a final verification step.

The methodology draws on practical experience with distributed systems design, contract-driven development, and the governance of AI-assisted engineering workflows. It is documented in peer-reviewed publications archived on Zenodo with persistent DOIs, and is available for use, citation, and adaptation under open access terms.
