IFEM Doctrine / About

Method identity

A technical doctrine for explicit system agreements.

IFEM Doctrine is the public methodology site for Interface-First Execution Methodology. It exists to make the methodology easier to understand, verify, cite, and apply.

Doctrine-firstPublication-ledImplementation-oriented

What this site is for

This site provides a direct technical introduction to IFEM and a clear route to its authoritative publications. It explains why interfaces, contracts, responsibility boundaries, and verification matter before parallel implementation begins. It is designed for technical visitors who need a concise mental model as well as a path to the full source material.

What this site is not

IFEM Doctrine is not a personal portfolio, a consulting brochure, a certification claim, or a collection of unsupported adoption stories. The methodology’s authority is grounded in its publication record, explicit concepts, diagrams, illustrative examples, and reproducible reasoning.

Author

Interface-First Execution Methodology (IFEM) is authored by Soheil Mozaffari, a computer scientist and systems architect working across software architecture, AI-assisted engineering, complex systems, and technical methodology design.
