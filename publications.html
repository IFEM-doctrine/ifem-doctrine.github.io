Citation guidance

Always cite the specific version of the publication you used. If you are citing the methodology in general, cite the most recent version of the primary publication. If you are citing a specific phase or concept, include the section reference in your citation. IFEM publications are versioned; earlier versions remain available on Zenodo with their original DOIs.
